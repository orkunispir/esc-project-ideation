# ESC output templates

Choose the smallest template that satisfies the request. Do not fill empty fields with invented prose.

## Concept set

```text
## Method
[method] — [originator/source as given by the method reference]

## Evidence base
- [source-backed anchor]
- [inference or gap]

## Grounded direction — [specific title]
**Mechanism:** [what participants do, with whom, where, and using what]
**Need/community:** [who and which situation]
**Activities:** [short sequence]
**Partners/roles:** [confirmed, plausible, or placeholder]
**Expected change:** [observable, modest outcome]
**Why it is not generic:** [exact departure from common shape or precedent]
**First validation step:** [7–14 day test]
**Failure mode/trade-off:** [what could make it fail]
**Evidence status:** [evidence / inference / hypothesis / open questions]
**Fit note:** [action/priority to verify, never an invented eligibility claim]

## Other directions
[Two or more materially different cards using the same fields, unless the method specifies another format.]
```

## Shortlist

```text
## Route
[method and why it fits the selection phase]

| Direction | Strongest evidence | Biggest dependency | Rubric trade-off | Next test |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Recommendation
[one choice, with the reason and what would reverse it]

### Pre-mortem
- [failure involving community/need]
- [failure involving partners or delivery]
- [failure involving safeguarding, evidence, or fit]
```

## Research question

```text
## Question
[one answerable question]

## Why this question matters
[what decision would change]

## Corpus contribution
[what the current corpus can reveal]

## What it cannot establish
[coverage, language, causality, local need, or eligibility limits]

## First study
[sample, comparison, data collection, or partner conversation]
```

## Concept note skeleton

Use only after a direction is selected. Write: context and need; target participants and community; solidarity objective; activity sequence; partner roles; inclusion/safeguarding/privacy; learning indicators; timeline; resource/budget assumptions; risks and mitigations; dissemination or transfer; open questions; source ledger. Mark every unverified programme rule as `to verify`.
