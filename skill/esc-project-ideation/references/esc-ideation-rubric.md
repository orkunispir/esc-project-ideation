# ESC ideation rubric

Use this as a decision aid after a creative method, not as an official grant scoring system. Score each dimension from 0–3 and explain the two decisive trade-offs.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Need and locality | Abstract or assumed | Broad need, weak location | Specific need and setting | Community-validated need with a clear place and timing |
| Solidarity mechanism | Attendance or content only | Vague volunteering | Concrete participant contribution | Reciprocal work shaped with the community |
| Participant/community fit | Roles absent | Roles generic | Roles and beneficiaries named | Power, accessibility, and safeguarding are designed in |
| Activity specificity | Category language | Activity named, mechanism unclear | Sequence, medium, and setting concrete | A small pilot could be described step by step |
| Partnership feasibility | Imagined partner | Partner type only | Plausible roles and dependencies | Named or testable partner pathway, with fallback |
| Evidence quality | Pure invention | Anecdote or weak analogy | Relevant records or observations | Multiple source types with limits stated |
| Learning value | No observable change | Output only | Outcome and indicator plausible | Test could change the project or future practice |
| Distinctiveness | Copy of a common shape | Minor novelty | Clear departure from precedent | Unusual mechanism that still serves the need |
| Delivery realism | No first step | Large plan, no test | Feasible pilot and dependencies | Time, capacity, safeguarding, and budget assumptions explicit |
| Ethical quality | Extractive or unsafe | Risks noticed late | Risks mitigated in design | Community agency, consent, privacy, and inclusion are structural |

## Interpretation

- A grounded direction should normally score at least 2 on need, solidarity mechanism, activity specificity, and delivery realism.
- A high total does not establish eligibility or approval probability.
- Any 0 in safeguarding, consent, privacy, or community agency is a stop-and-rework signal, not something to average away.
- A lower-scoring unusual direction may be worth a validation sprint if its missing evidence is cheap to obtain and the mechanism is promising.

## Evidence labels

Use these labels in output:

- **Evidence:** directly stated by the user or returned by a source.
- **Inference:** a pattern derived from one or more evidence items.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposed explanation or opportunity that still needs validation.
- **Open question:** a missing fact that could materially change the concept.
